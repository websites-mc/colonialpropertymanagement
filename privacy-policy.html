Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 9, 2026

Colonial Property Management, LLC ("Colonial Property Management," "we," "us," or "our") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information, including in connection with our SMS/text messaging program.

Information We Collect

  • Contact Information: Your name, email address, mailing address, and phone number when you submit a form on our website, contact our offices, register for tenant or owner web access, submit a maintenance request, request a showing, or otherwise interact with our services.
  • Mobile Phone Number: If you choose to opt in to our text messaging program, we collect your mobile phone number for the purpose of sending and receiving text messages.
  • User-Provided Information: Any additional information you voluntarily provide, which helps us improve our services and provide better support.

How We Use Your Information

  • Deliver text messages and updates related to our services, including customer care responses, rent and account reminders, maintenance updates, urgent notifications, community news, and events.
  • Send occasional marketing and promotional messages, where you have provided express consent.
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support.
  • Improve our services and user experience.

SMS/Text Messaging Privacy

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third parties for any purpose.

Your consent to receive text messages from Colonial Property Management, LLC applies only to our messaging program and is not transferable or assignable to any other party or program.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers only as essential to operate our business (for example, our property management software platform), and such providers may not use your information for their own marketing purposes.

Data Security

We take reasonable physical, administrative, and technical steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access and disclosure. However, no data transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information.

Opting Out of Text Messages

If you wish to be removed from receiving future text communications, you can opt out at any time by replying STOP, QUIT, END, REVOKE, OPT OUT, CANCEL, or UNSUBSCRIBE to any message. You may also contact us by phone or email to be removed. Reply HELP to any message for assistance.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our data practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated version on our website. Your continued use of our services after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.
